B2B Cross-Sell Playbook: 8 Campaigns to Convert the Base

Every SOHO and Small Business account maps to one of eight profiles based on what it holds and bills today. This playbook turns each group into a campaign: the target offer, the team that owns it, and a two-phase outreach motion — a digital warm-up followed by a human touch — with measurement and responder exclusion built in.

What changed since launch

Updates applied across this playbook as of October 1, 2026.

  • Two phases, not four. The program runs Digital Warm-Up → Human Touch. The Progressive Dialer hasn’t launched, so it’s been removed, along with the separate Sustain & Learn phase.
  • Leads are split by what each team sells. The SOHO Team works mobile-only customers to sell fixed (Campaigns 1, 2, 6, 7). The Contact Center works fixed-only customers to sell mobile (Campaigns 3, 4, 5, 8). Both teams resumed work on the re-split leads in the first week of September.
  • Authorized retailers joined the SOHO Team. Connection Expert and Express Cellular work Campaigns 1, 2, 6 and 7 door-to-door from their own Salesforce lead reports. Escalations go to Edwin Girald.
  • All 8 offers are confirmed. Campaigns 1–2 are $81.49/mo (no promo price). Campaigns 6–7 are Liberty Business in a Box at $211/mo. Campaign 8 is MIX Business Ultra, 6 lines at $43/mo each with autopay.
  • One disposition flow for every Lead. Qualifying before the call, one outcome after it, and a Chatter comment every time — see the Process tab.
  • Scripts live in Salesforce Knowledge. The Word scripts with the old $84.99 price and dialer steps are retired; each campaign links to its Knowledge article.

How to read each campaign: the left card is the account’s profile today and the right card is the offer we lead with. Every campaign runs the same two-phase motion: Phase 1 is a digital touch (email, plus a self-service form where one exists) that creates Salesforce Leads, and Phase 2 is a human call or visit from the team that owns the campaign. Customers who already responded in Phase 1 are excluded from Phase 2 through their Lead Status.

Program cadence

Phase 1 sends on Day 0, reminds on Day 5 and closes on Day 10. Human Touch starts on Day 11 and stays open until every Lead has an outcome.
